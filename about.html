OUR PASSION

MORE THAN JUST MINIATURE CARS

Authan Mini Garage is a miniature automotive space based in Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu.

Our passion is simple — cars. We bring the excitement of the automotive world into a miniature scale through diecast cars, car toys and detailed dioramas.

From collectors looking for their next miniature model to enthusiasts who enjoy creative car displays, Authan Mini Garage aims to create a place where every car lover can enjoy the details of miniature automotive culture.
